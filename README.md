# Tadarab Jo | تدرب جو

![Tadarab Jo Overview](assets/tadarab-jo-overview.png)

## Overview

Tadarab Jo is an integrated platform designed to streamline university students' field-training and professional development journey.

The platform connects students, universities, approved training providers, and platform administrators through a centralized system that simplifies training applications, approval workflows, and access to professional development opportunities.

## Platform Tracks

### 1. Mandatory Field Training

The field-training track is designed to facilitate the mandatory practical training process required by universities.

Students can:

* Discover training opportunities from companies approved by their universities.
* Submit training applications remotely.
* Follow the required approval workflow between the university and the training provider.
* Manage the training process without repeatedly visiting different parties for paperwork and approvals.

The platform is designed to reduce the administrative and logistical burden associated with traditional paper-based field-training procedures, helping save time and effort for students, universities, and training providers.

### 2. Courses & Training Sessions

The second track focuses on professional development by bringing together courses, workshops, and training sessions that help university students develop skills relevant to the future job market.

## AI Features

* AI-powered CV analysis using the Groq API.
* In-app AI assistant integrated into the platform.
* Shared AI session between CV analysis and the AI assistant to maintain contextual interactions within the application.

## Stakeholders

* University
* Student
* Training Provider
* Platform Administrator

## Core Concept

Tadarab Jo aims to replace repetitive, paper-based training procedures with a centralized digital workflow.

Instead of repeatedly moving between the university and training provider to collect documents and approvals, students can initiate and manage the training process remotely through the platform.

The platform also provides centralized administrative control through a dedicated dashboard.
